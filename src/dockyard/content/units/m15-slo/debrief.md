# Debrief: Measure a service-level objective honestly

An SLO calculation depends on an explicit population of requests, a success definition, and an observation window.
The observed ratio is meaningful only when its numerator and denominator describe the same service boundary.

## Explain your result

Explain which requests your indicator includes, which it excludes, and how missing observations could make the result misleading.

## Transfer beyond this lab

A short local rehearsal demonstrates the calculation but cannot establish a long-term production reliability commitment.
