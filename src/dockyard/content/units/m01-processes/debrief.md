## What just happened?

You ran the first version of Dispatch, the application you’ll grow for the rest of the course.
The image supplied Python, the bind mount supplied Dispatch’s code, and `python app.py` was the container’s main process.
The port mapping carried your request from your computer to that process.

Check your understanding:
Which of Dispatch’s four parts did you run today, and which three are still to come?
What would happen to the health request if you removed the container but kept the image?
If either answer feels uncertain, look back at the figures in Understand.
